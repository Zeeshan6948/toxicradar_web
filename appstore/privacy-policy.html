What the Apple Watch app collects

With your permission, the ToxicRadar Watch app reads from Apple Health:

  • Heart rate (live, while monitoring is switched on)
  • Heart rate variability (HRV)
  • Resting heart rate

It also uses the watch motion sensor to tell whether you are sitting still. Motion data is processed on the watch and only a still/moving flag is sent.

How it is used

This data is used only to show your current stress level next to the message you are writing in ToxicRadar, so you can notice when you may be writing while stressed or angry. It is not used for advertising, marketing, profiling, or any other purpose.

What we do NOT do

  • We do not sell or share health data with third parties, advertisers, data brokers, or insurers.
  • We do not store health data in iCloud.
  • We do not write any workout or health records back to Apple Health.

Storage and retention

Readings are sent over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection to your ToxicRadar session and kept in server memory only. At most the 500 most recent readings per session are kept. Data is deleted when you disconnect the watch, when the session is idle for 24 hours, or when the server restarts. Pairing codes expire after 10 minutes.

Your control

You can stop monitoring at any time on the watch, tap "Unpair" to remove the connection, and revoke Health access in Settings > Privacy & Security > Health on your iPhone.

Message content

Text you analyze in ToxicRadar is sent to OpenAI to produce tone analysis and rewrite suggestions. Heart-rate data is not included in that request.
